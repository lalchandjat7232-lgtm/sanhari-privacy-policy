Account Deletion Request

SanHari Enterprises

You can request deletion of your SanHari Enterprises account and the personal data associated with it by following the instructions on this page.

Important: Sending a request does not delete your account automatically. Our team will review your request and contact you if additional information is required to verify your identity.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to our support address using the button below.
  2. Include your registered mobile number and state that you want your SanHari Enterprises account deleted.
  3. Do not include your password or any other login credentials.
  4. Our team will verify your request and process it securely.
Request Account Deletion

What Data Will Be Deleted?

After verifying your request, we will delete your account and associated personal data that is eligible for deletion, subject to applicable legal and business recordkeeping requirements.

Depending on the services you have used, this may include account details, contact information, and personal profile information associated with your account.

What Data May Be Retained?

Certain business, billing, transaction, or financial records may need to be retained where required by applicable law or legitimate recordkeeping obligations. Such records will be retained only as necessary and handled appropriately.

Processing Time

We aim to review and process verified deletion requests within 30 days. If additional verification or legally required retention applies, we will communicate the relevant details.
