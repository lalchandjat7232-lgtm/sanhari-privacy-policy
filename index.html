Privacy Policy

SanHari Enterprises

Last updated: October 9, 2026

SanHari Enterprises respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, the application may process:

  • Account and login information.
  • Name, phone number, and business or retailer details.
  • Product names, prices, stock, categories, and inventory records.
  • Order, billing, purchase, and transaction records entered into the app.
  • Product photographs or other images you choose to upload.
  • Information you provide when contacting us for support.

The information processed depends on the features enabled in your version of the application. Please do not enter unnecessary sensitive personal information into the app.

2. Camera and Photo Access

If you use an image upload feature, the application may request access to your camera or photos, depending on your device and the feature selected. These permissions are used to let you capture or select images, such as product photographs.

You can decline these permissions, although certain image-related features may then be unavailable.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide account access and application functionality.
  • Manage products, inventory, retailers, orders, and bills.
  • Save and retrieve business records.
  • Display product photographs and related information.
  • Maintain security, troubleshoot problems, and provide support.

4. Data Storage and Service Providers

The application may use local device storage and Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, where configured, to support login and data storage.

Information stored locally may remain on your device. Information stored through online services may be processed by those service providers according to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

The actual storage method depends on the feature and configuration used by the application.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate the application, or disclosed when required by law or when necessary to protect the rights and security of users and the service.

If you use a feature that shares a bill, product, or other information through another application, the information you choose to share may be handled according to that application's privacy practices.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Business records may be retained for as long as needed to provide the service or meet applicable legal and business requirements. Some records may remain in device storage or online backups until they are removed.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your use of the application, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before processing a request.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Users should protect their devices and account credentials.

8. Children's Privacy

This application is intended for business use and is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
